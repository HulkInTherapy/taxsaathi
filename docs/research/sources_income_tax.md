## Contradictions and corrections to the background facts
1. **31 August due date already applies to FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27).** The background said "possibly 31 Aug 2027 for TY 2026-27". In fact the official ITR-4 FAQ gives 31 August 2026 for AY 2026-27, and the date continues for TY 2026-27 under s.263(1).
2. **Revised-return deadline moved to 31 March from AY 2026-27**, with a s.234I fee (₹1,000/₹5,000) after 31 December (official ITR-1 FAQ). Under the 2025 Act the fee sits in s.428(b).
3. **Audit-case due date for AY 2026-27 was extended by CBDT to 21 November 2026** (from 31 October).
4. **ITR-1/ITR-4 now allow two house properties** (from AY 2026-27).
5. **FY 2024-25 rebate:** the statutory cap is ₹25,000, but one official e-filing page (AY 2025-26 salaried help) says "₹20,000". That figure is the slab tax at ₹7L, not the cap.
6. **s.202(2) text** says Chapter VIII deductions are barred except ss.124(1), 124(2), 125(2) and 146. The small fetch model also read s.19(1) Table Sl. No. 1 as disallowed, which contradicts s.19 itself (₹75,000 where tax is computed under s.202(1)). The KB follows s.19.
7. **206AA→397** could not be confirmed and may be wrong.

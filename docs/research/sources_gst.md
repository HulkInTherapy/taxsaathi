## Contradictions with the background brief

1. **Refrigerators and washing machines did NOT go from 28% to 18% in GST 2.0.** The PIB annexure has no entry for HSN 8418 (refrigerators) or 8450 (washing machines). Both were already cut to 18% from 27 July 2018 (28th Council). Taxscan published a fact-check correcting news outlets that said otherwise. The GST 2.0 appliance cuts were for ACs, dishwashers and TVs above 32 inches. Gold QG043 ("washing machine in July 2025" → 18%) tests exactly this.
2. **Tobacco and pan masala did not move to 40% on 22 Sep 2025.** They stayed at 28% plus compensation cess until 31 Jan 2026 and moved to 40% (bidi to 18%) on 1 Feb 2026, when the cess became nil. HSNS cess and additional excise now sit outside GST. For all other goods, cess ended on 22 Sep 2025.
3. **Bidi goes to 18%, not 40%.**
4. **GSTR-4 due date is 30 June** (rule 62 proviso, from FY 2024-25 onwards), not the older 30 April.
5. **The services composition (6%) now sits in section 10(2A) and rule 7.** Notification 2/2019-CT(R) was the original 2019 route. The rate and the Rs 50 lakh limit are unchanged.
6. **Composition dealers are no longer barred from all e-commerce sales.** Since 1 Oct 2023 they can sell goods intra-state through TCS-collecting e-commerce operators (Notification 36/2023-CT). Supplying services through ECOs is still barred. The CBIC consolidated text of s.10(2)(d) still shows "goods or services", and its footnotes look misplaced, so the chunk is marked partial.
7. **The rule text gives each composition rate as CGST only** (0.5% / 2.5% / 3%). The 1% / 5% / 6% figures in the brief are the combined CGST+SGST totals, which is correct. A search tool summary misread this as "manufacturers 0.5%".
8. **There are more 40% items than the brief lists:** caffeinated beverages, carbonated fruit drinks, personal aircraft, yachts, betting, casinos, lottery, online money gaming, and admission to the IPL, casinos and race clubs. Plain unsweetened mineral water fell from 18% to 5%.
9. **Hotels (≤Rs 7,500) and salons/gyms are 5% "without ITC".** The brief doesn't mention the ITC condition.
10. **New in 2025–26, not in the brief:** Rule 14A simplified registration (1 Nov 2025); GSTR-9 exemption up to Rs 2 crore made standing from FY 2024-25 (Notification 15/2025-CT); GSTR-3B interest auto-computation from Jan 2026; the 3-year time bar on returns; Finance Act 2026 amendments (none to thresholds). **No 2026 change to registration thresholds, composition limits, QRMP or the e-invoice threshold was found.**

## Unverifiable or weaker items
- The exact compensation cess percentages on cars and aerated drinks before GST 2.0 are left out of the KB on purpose, because they couldn't be confirmed against an official source.
- The text of the GST rate notifications for 1 Feb 2026 (Notification 19/2025-CT(R) and 01/2025-Comp Cess (Rate)) was not read directly. The numbers come from wire and tax-commentary sources. The PIB HSNS FAQ officially confirms the 1 Feb 2026 start date.
- The Notification 18/2025-CT number for Rule 14A comes from secondary sources. The Rule 14A page on taxinformation.cbic.gov.in did not load at the URL pattern tried.
- Late-fee caps: the notification tables give per-Act (CGST) amounts. The KB states combined CGST+SGST totals, following the 43rd Council summary.
- Some Rs 40 lakh opt-in states (for example Telangana) may have changed their state-level choice. This was not re-verified for 2026.
- The CLAUDE.md Tavily workflow could not be used: there is no `.env` with Tavily keys in ~/taxsaathi. The built-in WebSearch/WebFetch tools and curl were used instead, as the task instructed. pib.gov.in blocks WebFetch (403), so its pages were fetched with curl.

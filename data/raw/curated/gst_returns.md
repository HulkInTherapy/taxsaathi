## [GST-RET-GSTR1] GSTR-1: statement of outward supplies and its due dates
section: 37, Rule 59
effective_from: 2021-01-01
effective_to:
topic: returns
verified: yes

GSTR-1 is the statement of outward supplies (sales) that every normal registered taxpayer files under section 37 of the CGST Act. It reports invoice-wise B2B sales, consolidated B2C sales, credit and debit notes, exports and an HSN-wise summary; the details flow to buyers' GSTR-2B so they can claim input tax credit. Monthly filers must file GSTR-1 by the 11th of the following month (for example, September 2026 sales by 11 October 2026). Taxpayers in the QRMP scheme file it quarterly by the 13th of the month after the quarter (for example, July to September 2026 by 13 October 2026). Under section 37(5), GSTR-1 cannot be filed after three years from its due date. Composition taxpayers do not file GSTR-1.

## [GST-RET-GSTR3B] GSTR-3B: monthly summary return and tax payment
section: 39, Rule 61
effective_from: 2021-01-01
effective_to:
topic: returns
verified: yes

GSTR-3B is the summary return under section 39 in which a regular taxpayer declares total outward supplies, input tax credit claimed, and pays the net tax. Monthly filers must file it by the 20th of the following month (rule 61). For QRMP taxpayers it is filed quarterly, by the 22nd of the month after the quarter for principal places of business in Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and some UTs, and by the 24th for all other states, such as Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Punjab and West Bengal. A return cannot be filed after three years from its due date (section 39(11)). From the January 2026 tax period the portal also auto-computes interest on late payment in Table 5.1 of GSTR-3B.

## [GST-RET-QRMP] QRMP scheme: quarterly returns with monthly tax payment
section: 39(1) proviso, Rule 61A
effective_from: 2021-01-01
effective_to:
topic: returns_qrmp
verified: partial

The Quarterly Return Monthly Payment (QRMP) scheme lets registered taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to Rs 5 crore in the preceding financial year file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly instead of monthly. Tax must still be paid every month: for the first two months of a quarter, tax is paid by the 25th of the next month through challan PMT-06, either by the fixed-sum method (35% of the last quarter's cash tax, generated by the portal) or by self-assessing actual liability. Tax for the third month is paid with the quarterly GSTR-3B. The optional Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) lets QRMP taxpayers upload B2B invoices for the first two months by the 13th of the next month, so their buyers can claim ITC earlier. QRMP suits small shopkeepers and freelancers with few B2B invoices.

## [GST-RET-GSTR9] GSTR-9 annual return and the Rs 2 crore exemption
section: 44, Rule 80
effective_from: 2017-07-01
effective_to:
topic: returns_annual
verified: partial

GSTR-9 is the annual return under section 44 of the CGST Act and rule 80, due by 31 December after the end of the financial year (for example, FY 2025-26 by 31 December 2026). Taxpayers with aggregate turnover above Rs 5 crore must also file GSTR-9C, a self-certified reconciliation statement. Small taxpayers are exempt: Notification 15/2025-Central Tax (17 September 2025) exempts registered persons whose aggregate turnover in a financial year is up to Rs 2 crore from filing the annual return for FY 2024-25 onwards; similar year-by-year exemptions were given for earlier years. Composition taxpayers file GSTR-4 instead. An annual return cannot be filed more than three years after its due date (section 44(2)).

## [GST-RET-LATEFEE] Late fees for GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-4 and GSTR-9
section: 47
effective_from: 2021-06-01
effective_to:
topic: returns_late_fee
verified: partial

Section 47 of the CGST Act levies a late fee for filing returns after the due date, reduced by notifications following the 43rd GST Council meeting. For GSTR-3B and GSTR-1, the fee is Rs 50 per day (Rs 25 CGST + Rs 25 SGST), or Rs 20 per day for nil returns, capped per return at Rs 500 for nil returns, Rs 2,000 if prior-year turnover is up to Rs 1.5 crore, Rs 5,000 for Rs 1.5-5 crore and Rs 10,000 above Rs 5 crore. For GSTR-4 (composition), the cap is Rs 500 for nil returns and Rs 2,000 otherwise. For GSTR-9, from FY 2022-23 the fee is Rs 50 per day for turnover up to Rs 5 crore and Rs 100 per day for Rs 5-20 crore, capped at 0.04% of state turnover (Notification 7/2023-CT). Interest at 18% a year is separately payable on late tax payment.

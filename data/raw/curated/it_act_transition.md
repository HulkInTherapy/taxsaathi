## [IT-ACT-COMMENCEMENT] Income-tax Act, 2025 comes into force on 1 April 2026
section: 1
law: new_act
effective_from: 2024-04-01
effective_to:
topic: transition
verified: yes

The Income-tax Act, 2025 (Act 30 of 2025) replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961. Parliament passed the Bill on 12 August 2025 and the President gave assent on 21 August 2025. Save as otherwise provided, the new Act came into force on 1 April 2026, and the 1961 Act stands repealed from that date (subject to the savings clause in section 536). The new Act is much shorter: 536 sections and 16 schedules, against 819 sections and 14 schedules in the 1961 Act. The Income-tax Rules, 2026 were notified on 20 March 2026 to operationalise the new Act, cutting the rules from 511 to 333 and the forms from 399 to 190, and new form numbers apply from 1 April 2026. The Budget 2026-27 changes for the year 2026-27 were made directly in the new Act through the Finance Act, 2026.

## [IT-ACT-TAXYEAR] "Tax Year" replaces previous year and assessment year (section 3, Income-tax Act 2025)
section: 3
law: new_act
effective_from: 2026-04-01
effective_to:
topic: transition
verified: yes

Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 the twin concepts of "previous year" (the year income is earned) and "assessment year" (the following year in which it is assessed) are replaced by a single concept, the "tax year", defined in section 3. A tax year is the twelve-month period of the financial year beginning on 1 April. Income earned from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 is therefore "Tax Year 2026-27"; there is no separate "AY 2027-28" label under the new Act. For a newly set-up business or a new source of income, the first tax year runs from the date it starts until the following 31 March. When reading older documents, FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27 under the 1961 Act is the last year that uses the old terminology.

## [IT-ACT-WHICH-LAW] Which Act applies to which income period (section 536 savings)
section: 536
law: general
effective_from: 2024-04-01
effective_to:
topic: transition
verified: yes

Which law applies depends on when the income was earned, not when the return is filed. Income earned up to 31 March 2026 (FY 2025-26 and earlier years) remains governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961 and is assessed under it (FY 2025-26 = AY 2026-27). Section 536(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 saves the 1961 Act for proceedings relating to years beginning before 1 April 2026, even if those proceedings start after that date. Income earned from 1 April 2026 onwards is governed by the Income-tax Act, 2025, starting with Tax Year 2026-27. For TDS, salary for March 2026 paid on 31 March 2026 falls under section 192 of the 1961 Act, while salary paid on or after 1 April 2026 falls under section 392 of the 2025 Act. So salary earned in February 2026 is taxed under the 1961 Act.

## [IT-ACT-RETURN-FY2526] Return for FY 2025-26 filed in 2026 uses 1961 Act forms (AY 2026-27)
section: 139
law: old_act
effective_from: 2025-04-01
effective_to: 2026-03-31
topic: transition
verified: yes

Even though the Income-tax Act, 2025 is in force from 1 April 2026, the income-tax return for FY 2025-26 (assessment year 2026-27), filed during 2026, is filed under section 139 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 using ITR forms prescribed under the 1961 Act and the Income-tax Rules, 1962. CBDT notified all seven ITR forms (ITR-1 to ITR-7), plus ITR-V and ITR-U, for AY 2026-27 around 30–31 March 2026. Deductions for this return are still claimed under the old section numbers such as 80C, 80D and 87A. The first return under the new Act will be for Tax Year 2026-27, to be filed in 2027 under section 263 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 with forms notified under the Income-tax Rules, 2026.

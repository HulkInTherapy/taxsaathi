## [IT-CESS] Health and Education Cess at 4% on tax plus surcharge
section: Finance Act s.3
effective_from: 2024-04-01
effective_to:
topic: cess
verified: yes

Every individual, under both the old and new regimes, pays a Health and Education Cess of 4% on the income tax (after rebate) plus any surcharge. It applied for FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26 through the annual Finance Acts, and section 3 of the Finance Act, 2026 continues the 4% cess for Tax Year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Example: if slab tax after rebate is ₹1,12,500 and there is no surcharge, cess is ₹4,500 and total tax is ₹1,17,000. If the rebate brings tax to zero, cess is also zero. Cess is part of the final tax and also has to be covered in advance tax and TDS.

## [IT-SURCHARGE] Surcharge rates for individuals: 10%, 15%, 25%, 37%
section: Finance Act s.3
effective_from: 2024-04-01
effective_to:
topic: surcharge
verified: yes

Surcharge is an extra levy on income tax for high earners. For individuals: nil up to ₹50 lakh total income; 10% above ₹50 lakh up to ₹1 crore; 15% above ₹1 crore up to ₹2 crore; 25% above ₹2 crore up to ₹5 crore; and above ₹5 crore, 37% under the old regime but capped at 25% under the new regime (section 115BAC / section 202). Surcharge on capital gains under sections 111A, 112 and 112A and on dividend income is capped at 15%. These rates applied in FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26 and continue for Tax Year 2026-27 under section 3 of the Finance Act, 2026. Cess of 4% is then charged on tax plus surcharge. Most first-jobbers and freelancers earning below ₹50 lakh pay no surcharge.

## [IT-SURCHARGE-MARGINAL] Marginal relief on surcharge at ₹50 lakh, ₹1 crore, ₹2 crore and ₹5 crore
section: Finance Act s.3
effective_from: 2024-04-01
effective_to:
topic: surcharge
verified: yes

Marginal relief prevents a small rise in income from causing a big jump in tax when you cross a surcharge threshold. If total income slightly exceeds ₹50 lakh, ₹1 crore, ₹2 crore or ₹5 crore, the tax plus surcharge cannot exceed the tax plus surcharge payable at exactly that threshold by more than the income above the threshold. Example: at ₹50,10,000, the extra tax caused by the 10% surcharge is limited to ₹10,000 more than the tax on ₹50 lakh. This is different from the rebate marginal relief at ₹12 lakh (new regime), which works under section 87A / section 156. The Finance Act, 2026 contains the same relief formula for Tax Year 2026-27.
